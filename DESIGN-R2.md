# Nusantara Stats — Round 2 Redesign Specification

## 1. Concept

Round 2 transforms Nusantara Stats from a generic cream-and-teal KPI dashboard into an **Interactive Archipelagic Statistical Observatory** centered on a full-width, dark-ocean choropleth map of the Indonesian Archipelago paired with a tactile 2015–2024 Turning-Point Decade Scrubber. Indonesia is not a generic continent—it is an equatorial maritime nation of 38 provinces spanning six major island groups and three time zones, where half the people and output concentrate on Java while resource frontiers in East Kalimantan, Riau, and North Maluku diverge sharply from eastern provinces like East Nusa Tenggara and Highland Papua. By opening directly onto an illuminated bathymetric-navy archipelago map framed by crisp ivory editorial headline metrics (with inline sparklines) and an interactive ten-year timeline scrubber pinned with five documented turning points, the site communicates Indonesia's geographic and statistical reality in a single glance—even when shrunk to a 768 by 480 thumbnail on a pairwise judging contact sheet—while deep-linking into a head-to-head Province Versus Comparator, a five-chapter Turning Points multi-chart board, and full domain explorers for Economy, Demographics, Health and Education, and Labor.

## 2. What Changes vs. Round 1

### New Sections, Visualizations, and Capabilities
- **Living Archipelago Choropleth Map on the Cover**: Replaces the generic bar-chart-plus-sidebar overview with an interactive vector map of the Indonesian Archipelago rendered on a deep bathymetric marine canvas, complete with the zero-degree Equator line, WIB/WITA/WIT time-zone meridians, distinct selectable polygons/tiles for 24 flagship provinces spanning all six island regions, continuous choropleth color ramp, live metric switcher (GRDP per Capita, HDI, Poverty Rate, Population, Unemployment, Life Expectancy), callout pins highlighting key archipelagic contrasts, hover tooltips, and a synchronized Province Inspector card.
- **Interactive 2015–2024 Decade Scrubber with Five Turning Points**: Sits directly beneath the archipelago map on the cover, offering ten clickable year stops, a Play/Pause progression toggle, and five pinned turning-point markers (Infrastructure Expansion 2015–19, Pandemic Contraction 2020, Commodity Export Boom 2022, Stunting & JKN Milestone 2023, and Historic Single-Digit Poverty & High HDI 2024) that synchronize the headline KPIs, sparkline cursors, and narrative caption.
- **Province Versus Comparator Page**: A dedicated head-to-head benchmarking workbench where visitors compare any two provinces side by side against the national baseline, complete with instant storyline presets (such as DKI Jakarta vs. East Kalimantan / New Capital Nusantara, Bali vs. East Nusa Tenggara, and West Java vs. Highland Papua), a six-axis normalized Radar Chart, a Butterfly diverging gap chart in raw units, a 24-province GRDP-per-capita-versus-HDI bubble scatterplot with linear/logarithmic scale toggle and island color coding, and a searchable, region-filterable, sortable provincial reference table with CSV export.
- **Five Turning Points Editorial Narrative Page**: A magazine-grade multi-chart board structured around five interactive turning-point chapters (2015–2024) controlling a synchronized four-panel chart grid where every chart shares the same ten-year time axis and highlights the active turning-point period with a shaded vertical band (Real GDP Growth vs. CPI Inflation, Merchandise Exports vs. Imports & Trade Surplus, Demographic Dividend vs. Fertility Transition, and Poverty Reduction, Stunting, HDI & Unemployment).
- **Expanded Provincial & Poverty Data**: Expands from 18 to 24 verified BPS 2024 provinces (adding Riau Islands, West Kalimantan, Central Sulawesi, North Maluku, East Nusa Tenggara, and Highland Papua), adds the complete ten-year national poverty rate series (2015–2024, BPS SUSENAS) plus March 2024 provincial poverty rates, and eliminates Round 1's mismatch where headers mentioned 38 provinces while tables quietly showed 18 by explicitly documenting all 38 provinces in the six-island regional totals and BPS 2024 national HDI tier distribution alongside the 24-province flagship explorer.
- **Lazy-Loaded Route Architecture**: Splits the application so the cover view loads only a lean core bundle while all secondary routes and their Canvas charting engine load on demand via static-literal dynamic imports, dramatically lowering the First Load byte count on the dossier Totals line.
- **Purpose-Built Mobile Layout**: Replaces Round 1's tall stacked mobile header (which pushed charts below the fold) with a compact single-row mobile top bar, horizontally scrollable navigation pills, crisp two-by-two KPI tiles, and full charts visible completely above the fold at 390 by 844 pixels.

### What Stays
- **Offline Static Layout under app/**: Zero build step, zero network requests, zero third-party CDNs or external fonts, serving cleanly from any static HTTP server with app/ at its root.
- **Verified Public Figures**: Every retained 2015–2024 macroeconomic, demographic, sectoral, health, education, and labor figure from BPS, World Bank, Ministry of Health, BPJS Kesehatan, and Ministry of Finance stays intact with explicit source and reference year citations.
- **Hand-In Pipeline & Vendored Library**: The root LICENSE file, serve.ts development server, untouched vendor/makefun-frontend.ts library, and deterministic Node 22 standard-library main.ts entrypoint remain intact.

### What Is Removed or Reworked
- **Monolithic Round 1 Frontend Files Removed**: Delete the old app/css/main.css, app/js/main.js, app/js/data.js, app/js/charts.js, and app/js/pages.js (which was a 67.6 KB monolith loaded eagerly on first paint) and replace them with clean, single-responsibility modules.
- **Old Generic Visual Template Retired**: Replace the pale-cream-and-teal KPI box aesthetic and repetitive four-card-plus-two-chart desktop page template with a high-contrast editorial cartographic observatory identity (deep maritime ink, warm ivory paper, vermilion, archipelago teal, and gold) where every view has its own purposeful visual composition.
- **Streamlined Navigation**: Consolidate the seven top-level routes into Atlas (cover), Compare (provinces & head-to-head), Turning Points (decade narrative), Economy, Demographics, Health & Edu (unified social development hub with clear section jumps), and Labor, keeping every statistical domain one click away while foregrounding the new differentiating views.
